# Domain glossary

- **Customer account**: authenticated user identity.
- **Customer profile**: business profile linked one-to-one with a customer account.
- **Farm/property**: permanent land or terrace identity reused across engagements.
- **Lead/service request**: commercial intake and coordination record; not a project.
- **Quote version**: immutable itemized commercial proposal revision.
- **Quote acceptance**: customer snapshot of one exact eligible quote version.
- **Invoice**: posted amount due created from an accepted quote.
- **Payment**: provider or manual money receipt attempt; not automatically an invoice.
- **Allocation**: amount from a verified payment applied to an invoice.
- **Project**: authorized delivery engagement created after commercial prerequisites.
- **Work order**: scheduled unit of accountable field work.
- **Proof**: worker evidence pending independent review.
- **Verified evidence**: proof accepted by a reviewer; customer publication is a separate future control.
- **Audit event**: append-only summary of a critical actor/action/entity change.
- **Webhook event**: signed provider notification stored for replay protection and reconciliation.
